# Correct a mistaken saved refund

Use this only when a saved refund was a mistake. Only an online owner can confirm it. The app keeps the original sale and refund, then records a separate correction.

## Before you begin

1. Check the customer receipt, refund reference, actual money and actual goods.
2. Account for the **entire original refund amount**. It must have been recovered from the customer or never handed out, and must be in the correcting drawer. A partial recovery cannot use this automatic undo.
3. Stop branch selling. Clear baskets and synchronize every branch cashier PC. Close other shifts. Open your own owner shift in the original branch.
4. If the original refund was valid but the returned goods later went missing, investigate the stock loss. Do not undo a valid refund to hide a shortage.

## On the website

1. Open **Sales & receipts** and select the original receipt. Choose **Partial refund** for its selected-quantity details, or **Refund details** for a receipt with an old full refund.
2. Choose **Check remaining quantities**. Under **Saved refund history**, find the mistaken refund and choose **Check mistaken refund**.
3. Read the cash amount and each stock change. The preview shows the saleable quantity to remove, held quantity to cancel, and before/after saleable balance.
4. If **Automatic correction blocked** appears, keep the original record and reconcile the evidence with the owner. There is no force-override button.
5. Choose **Authorize refund correction**. Type the full cash recovered or retained in pesos. Add a unique document reference and a reason describing the mistake and the evidence (at least ten characters).
6. Check all three confirmations for cash, goods and synchronized PCs. Confirm once.
7. Wait for **Original refund undone**. Count the actual drawer and check the physical goods against the recorded changes. Keep signed cash and goods evidence with the correction reference.
8. If the customer still needs a different refund, choose **Prepare corrected refund**. The original selection is copied as a draft. Correct its quantities and physical disposition, review the new cash amount and save the linked replacement. Give only the confirmed new amount once.

## Example

An original sale was ₱880. A mistaken refund paid ₱365 and credited 2 kg of saleable feeds, 3 kg of held feeds and one saleable packet. The owner accounts for all ₱365 and checks that the credited goods are not store-owned customer returns to keep.

The correction adds ₱365 to the current drawer, removes the 2 kg and one packet stock credits, and cancels the untouched 3 kg held return. The receipt becomes refundable again. A correct replacement of ₱215 then pays only ₱215, with only its actual inspected goods entering stock. The financial result is ₱665 retained from the original sale. With a ₱100 opening float and no other activity, the drawer closes at ₱765.

**Net cash refunds** shows paid refunds minus recovered correction cash. Recovered cash is reported separately; it is not a new sale. A correction in a new shift adds cash to that new drawer. It never rewrites a closed earlier drawer, so keep the actual handover evidence when reconciling shifts.

## Why a correction may be blocked

- A later active refund exists on the same receipt. Correct the newest active refund first; this preserves the original centavo allocation.
- Saleable returned stock has later movements, even if its balance later returns to the same number, or a later approved physical count exists.
- Held goods were released, disposed of, returned to a supplier or otherwise processed.
- Return investigation work has already begun, or original cash/physical-return evidence is incomplete or inconsistent.
- Another branch cashier shift is open, the checked stock/refund history changed, or there is not enough saleable stock to remove the original credit.
- An old full refund has no complete physical-return evidence. Missing evidence is displayed as **not recorded**, not assumed to be zero.

A mistaken full refund can be undone when its evidence is complete and untouched. Any new refund on that receipt then uses selected quantities. Each correction can have one directly linked replacement. Further mistakes require their own recorded correction.

## If the connection or confirmation fails

Do not repeat the cash handover or create another correction. Open **Sync center**, export the recovery file and retry the **original request** after signing in again. Its permanent ID prevents duplicate cash and stock effects, including after the original drawer closes. Preserve unresolved records and the recovery file; do not clear browser storage. The app records staff attestations but cannot verify physical cash, goods or an unsynchronized second PC by itself.
