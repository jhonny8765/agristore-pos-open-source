# Blind stock counts

1. Synchronize every cashier PC and close every branch shift. Establish a physical cutoff. Separate held goods and stock in transit.
2. A branch-authorized stock-staff, manager or owner account starts a blind count for a product and lot. Enter physical base quantity and notes, then seal the first entry.
3. Another person signs in with their own account and independently counts the goods. The first quantity/notes and expected stock are not returned until the second entry is sealed. Entry mode does not display comparisons or count history.
4. An owner who entered neither count reviews both sealed observations and the expected balance. Matching current observations can be approved with evidence. This requires three separate account identities; if an owner counts, another owner must approve.
5. Investigate any discrepancy separately. Adjustment approval changes quantity once and retains the original evidence; it does not prove the cause or automatically resolve an investigation.

Disagreement, intervening branch stock movements, open shifts and revoked counter access block approval. Preserve the record, close it with a reason and start a fresh count as appropriate. Sealed entries cannot be edited. Original request retries retain their IDs; changed contents conflict. Pending legacy counts cannot use typed witness names to bypass the new procedure.

Count net kg, liters or whole pieces; tare containers and use documented pack conversions. Do not share passwords. The app separates account identities but cannot prove different humans used them or prevent authorized staff from looking up stock elsewhere. Physical independence remains an operating requirement.

The release uses a conservative branch-wide ledger revision: a movement in another product also invalidates an unfinished count. All differences require an independent matching recount; there is no materiality override. A zero-difference approval records evidence without a stock movement.

If a response is interrupted, use Sync center → Retry original request as the original employee. To recover on a replacement test profile, export the recovery file, sign in to the same business/branch, restore it and retry the retained request. Operation-only archives can be restored with shifts closed; archives containing sales still require an authorized cashier shift. The original employee identity and request IDs are preserved. An owner can retain another employee's evidence for review but cannot retry as that employee. Keep original evidence until confirmation is independently checked.
