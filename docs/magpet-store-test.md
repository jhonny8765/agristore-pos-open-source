# Magpet Branch: the first store test

Use **TEST products and simulated cash**. This sheet records the checks that cannot be established by software tests on another machine. Keep Magpet as the only enabled branch. The maintainer enables another branch only after the owner accepts the Magpet results.

Open the current website online in its dedicated normal browser profile. Keep its address and site data. Finish unfinished work and confirm/export any pending original records before updating. Use Settings → Check for app update, then reopen as instructed. The header must say **Magpet Branch**. Adding/renaming branches and transfer navigation must be unavailable.

The owner/manager registers this PC using a setup shift and closes that shift. The cashier then signs in with their own branch-limited account. Do not share the owner's password. Download the catalog and open the cashier's shift online with the actual simulated opening cash.

## Sell and verify

Write the starting stock, unit conversions, prices, lots/expiry and cash on a separate sheet. Confirm scale tare and the real measurements before saving. Include:

| Purchase | Check |
|---|---|
| 1/4 kg and 0.5 kg | Correct retail kilo price, cash change and stock reduction |
| 100 g or 250 g | Same exact quantity when switching between grams and kg |
| A peso request | Enter the actual scale reading; the final charge follows that measurement |
| Rope meters, liquids in liter/ml, whole pieces | Correct unit and pack conversion; no fractional sealed pieces |
| Mistake before saving | Edit/remove the basket item; do not create a false refund |
| Two identical purchases | Two different saved references |
| Reprint | Same original reference; no new sale or stock deduction |

Record at least **200 completed TEST sales**, including a representative busy-day burst toward the store's reported 300+ sales. Record the expected total cash and measured quantity independently; software does not validate a wrong physical scale or opening count.

## Check an actual outage and restart

1. Record disconnect time: __________. Turn off the real network. Do not change the clock.
2. Each completed purchase must show Sale saved and appear in Sync center. A failed save is not a completed sale. Do not hand over goods based on it.
3. Export every numbered recovery part while records are pending. Verify the files arrived and retain a protected separate copy. Keep the original profile.
4. Remain disconnected for at least **60 actual minutes**. Close/reopen the installed app/browser and perform one normal Windows restart after other work is saved. Compare every saved reference and amount.
5. Continue the test for **eight actual hours**, inside the displayed 12-hour offline authorization window. End time: __________. Record every additional test sale separately.
6. Reconnect, renew the original cashier's sign-in if needed, and confirm the original records. Large queues drain in batches; a 300-sale backlog can take several minutes even with a healthy connection. Watch progress and retained errors in Sync center. Do not re-enter purchases to repair uncertainty. The maintainer compares all original IDs/payloads, payments and stock movements.

The automated 300-sale exercise is a short synthetic test. It does not substitute for these elapsed-time, power, Windows, printer and scale checks.

## Rehearse the owner controls

- Receive an approved purchase order, including a short/rejected delivery and held goods. Only accepted saleable goods increase available stock.
- Try a guarded delivery correction before later activity; confirm a dependent correction is blocked rather than forced.
- Refund only part of an original purchase, recording actual returned/saleable/held goods. Reconcile the cash. Rehearse the guarded mistaken-refund correction with fully recovered cash.
- Record a measured damage/shortage request. The cashier must not approve it. Keep held goods physically separate; disposal must not deduct saleable stock twice.
- Close shifts, synchronize and freeze stock movements before a blind count. Two separate people enter independent counts; a third approving owner reviews. Preserve disagreements and investigations.
- Compare stock differences using documented purchase cost. Unknown cost remains unvalued, not zero. A shortage does not establish theft.

Close the cashier shift online only after the basket and original requests/sales are confirmed. Record the expected and counted cash without hiding differences using invented sales, refunds or stock adjustments.

A missing completed sale, false saved-success, duplicate cash/stock effect or failed storage recovery fails the pilot. Stop the affected workflow, preserve the profile/files and contact the maintainer.

Before customer use, separately finish verified real opening stock/costs, individual staff rehearsal, production hosting, scheduled encrypted off-site backups and an actual isolated backup restoration. Software tests do not establish these. During the one-branch pilot, sign daily cash and stock reconciliations. Expand only after the owner accepts the evidence and there are no unresolved completed-sale or stock/cash correctness failures.
