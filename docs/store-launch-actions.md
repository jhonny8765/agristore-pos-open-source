# Before Better Yield uses real money and stock

The current site is a TEST/staging business. The training PowerPoint is a guide, not approval to start live selling. The website maintainer runs the software; the store owner controls staff, physical stock and daily cash review.

## What the owner needs to prepare

1. **Choose one pilot branch and its Windows cashier PC.** Keep one dedicated normal browser profile and the same website address. Do not clear its site data. Record the PC, scale and printer models.
2. **Reserve a supervised test day.** Use labelled TEST goods and simulated cash. Complete 200 sales, a real 60-minute outage, eight actual hours, app/browser reopening and one normal Windows restart. Compare every original reference, payment and stock effect after reconnecting. Test representative busy-day bursts as well. The long test is currently postponed; it has not passed.
3. **Check the real equipment.** Tare and test the scale; sell quarter/half kilos, grams and peso requests confirmed by measured weight. Check rope meters, liter/ml measures and whole pieces. Print and read an original receipt and a reprint.
4. **Prepare trustworthy opening data.** Independently count each branch/product/lot. Document sack/container conversions, expiry and purchase cost. Separate damaged/held goods and in-transit stock. Import each opening once into production; TEST balances are not real opening stock.
5. **Name the staff and reviewers.** Each employee uses their own branch-limited account. Rehearse receiving, rejected goods, transfers, held goods and partial refunds. Blind counts need two different counters and a third approving owner. Assign a daily cash/stock reviewer and a contact for technical problems.

## What the maintainer and account owner must finish together

6. **Establish the production environment.** Record the intended website and database, suitable capacity and service terms, and how staging stays separate. Agree any service cost before enabling paid settings. No paid change is authorized or started by these fixes.
7. **Make a real recoverable backup.** Choose protected off-site storage, encryption-key custody, retention and schedule. Agree how much recent data loss is acceptable and how quickly the store must recover. Restore an actual scheduled production backup into an isolated working system and compare sales, payments, inventory ledger/projection, holds, transfers and audit evidence. A disposable test restore is not this evidence.
8. **Complete monitoring.** Database-failure checks and their test email are verified. Protected staging prevents normal external website checks; full production-site and missed-backup/missed-monitor alerts still need configuration. Check the last successful monitor and backup daily.

## Start with one branch

9. **Run a controlled pilot.** Sign daily physical cash and stock reconciliations. Keep every discrepancy assigned and investigated. Expand to other branches only when the owner accepts the results.
10. **Stop the affected workflow if a completed sale disappears or cash/stock is duplicated.** Preserve the original profile and every recovery-file part. Do not re-enter the purchase, clear storage, or invent an adjustment. Ask the maintainer to reconcile original references.

Official tax-invoice issuance, customer credit, electronic payments and full accounting remain outside the first pilot. The software cannot establish whether a historical shortage was theft or prevent every physical stock loss.
