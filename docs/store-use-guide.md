# Store-use guide

Open **Settings → PC checks & store guide** for the same procedures inside the application. They remain available with the cached application; online-only actions still require connectivity, current access and their usual permissions.

The PC checks read all unresolved sales/requests in your permitted view, warn about other-account records without exposing their contents, show saved catalog time and remaining authorization, and report available browser storage/cache observations. Refresh after another window saves. A failed local check requires preserving records and asking for help, not clearing site data.

An empty queue, browser persistence or active cache does not certify launch readiness. Before live use: choose the real PC and profile; pass actual outage/restart/endurance tests; check scales and printer; independently verify opening quantities, units, lots and documented costs; rehearse separate staff accounts and independent approval; restore a real production backup; and reconcile a one-branch pilot before expanding. These are cash-only pilot workflows; credit, electronic payments, full accounting and official tax invoices are outside the agreed release.

## Open the store and begin a shift

1. Confirm the branch, your individual account, actual drawer opening cash and checked scale/tare. Use the dedicated Edge profile.
2. Refresh stock and catalog online. Review expiry, held goods, unresolved investigations and outstanding transfers in Inventory.
3. Open your own cashier shift online and check authorization time. Stock staff use their own stock role. PC checks do not certify physical opening stock or hardware.

## Sell kilos, grams, peso amounts, meters, ml or pieces

1. Choose the actual product, lot and configured selling unit. Read the displayed conversion and price; a sealed bottle or packet may be sold by whole piece instead of loose contents.
2. Use kg/grams or liter/ml switches for measured goods and fractional meters for rope. For a peso request, weigh the goods and confirm the actual weight before taking the final amount. Never invent a pack conversion.
3. Confirm cash, amount due and change. Hand over goods only after Sale saved. Two separate identical purchases need separate sale references.

## A sale fails, freezes or has no clear result

1. A failed save is not a receipt. Keep the basket and inspect Sync center for a completed record before doing anything again.
2. If a completed sale exists, retain its reference and retry the original sale when needed. Do not enter a new purchase to force confirmation.
3. If storage failed or records are missing, stop checkout, preserve the profile and recovery evidence, and ask the owner/maintainer to investigate before more goods leave.

## Internet fails, power goes off or Windows restarts

1. Only the current authorized cashier can resume the same shift offline within its displayed authorization period. New sign-ins, shift opening/closure, refunds and stock changes need connectivity.
2. Reopen the same installed app and Edge profile after a restart. Check every completed receipt and the remaining authorization. If the app or records cannot be recovered, stop and preserve the PC.
3. Reconnect and sign in again if required. Let the original queue synchronize; verify cash and stock before closing. Never change the clock to extend authorization or repeat pending sales.

## Sign-in expires, access is revoked or a staff member leaves

1. Keep saved records and any unfinished basket details. Reconnect and sign in as the original employee when renewed authentication is required. Signing out clears temporary baskets, while saved records remain.
2. If access is refused, ask the owner to verify the employee's role and branch. Revocation cannot instantly reach a disconnected PC; collect the device and recovery evidence when staff leave.
3. Retry original requests through Sync center only under their authorized identity. Use the owner's documented recovery process for records from an unavailable employee; do not share an owner password or reuse another cashier's shift.

## Receive a delivery, including partial or rejected goods

1. Match the physical goods and supplier document to the correct owner-approved purchase order. Record accepted, held and rejected quantities separately using the real pack conversion and lot/expiry.
2. Only accepted goods become saleable. Keep held/rejected goods separate; retain supplier shortfall, return and credit references. A supplier claim note does not add stock or settle an account.
3. Inspect replacement deliveries separately. Do not record the same physical delivery again through quick receiving. If the response is lost, retry its original request in Sync center.

## A saved delivery or opening import is wrong

1. Stop dependent activity and synchronize every branch PC. For an untouched inspected delivery, the owner uses the guarded reversal preview and linked correction.
2. Later sales, transfers, counts, held outcomes, supplier activity and deliberate order closures may block reversal. Older quick receipts and opening imports require a documented owner reconciliation.
3. Retain the original and dependent records. Do not force an undo, import stock twice or invent a delivery to correct the balance. Use independent physical counts and documented approved adjustments where appropriate.

## Move stock to another branch; some goods are missing

1. Dispatch the exact product, lot and quantity. The destination records only what physically arrives, separating saleable and held/damaged portions.
2. Unreceived goods remain in transit. Compare dispatch and receipt documents, seals, weights and staff handover evidence.
3. An owner records a transfer loss only after investigation. Never dispatch again to repair a missing receipt, receive goods that did not arrive or deduct the origin twice.

## Goods expire, spill, break or need inspection

1. Segregate and label the actual goods. Submit the exact measured quantity, category and evidence as a stock issue; no stock effect posts until owner approval.
2. An owner may reject a mistaken pending request with a reason and permanent history. Rejection does not change stock or close an investigation; an approved request cannot be rejected to undo it.
3. For held goods, the owner records inspected release, disposal or supplier return. Disposal does not deduct saleable stock twice. Expired goods cannot be released for sale. Do not also adjust the same shortage through a count.

## Physical stock differs, or the two counters disagree

1. Synchronize all PCs, close shifts and stop branch movements at a recorded cutoff. Separate saleable, held and in-transit goods.
2. Two different people sign in and seal independent blind observations. An owner who entered neither reviews the sealed pair; disagreement or intervening movements require a fresh count.
3. Keep the original observations, count sheets and investigation. An approved adjustment reconciles the book quantity; it does not establish theft or the cause of a loss.

## Review stock losses and missing purchase costs

1. Open Overview's purchase-cost loss report. Check branch, dates, category and printed snapshot time. Unvalued rows mean the subtotal is incomplete, not that the missing goods cost zero.
2. Use a supplier or opening-stock document for the cost of one selected purchase unit. Check its conversion and value preview. Corrections retain earlier evidence; supplier returns and authorized use are shown separately from losses.
3. Load all required pages before exporting rows; CSV includes only loaded records while displayed full-range totals cover the complete filter. Investigate repeated differences by product, lot, staff handover and supplier without assuming a cause.

## Refund part of a sale or correct a mistaken refund

1. Find the original receipt. The online owner uses their own authorized drawer/shift, inspects actual returned goods and refunds only the selected remaining original quantities.
2. Record financial quantities separately from goods physically returned. Only verified saleable returns re-enter saleable stock; damaged/uninspected goods are held. Record the cash handover. Do not use a full refund after partial refunds.
3. For the latest untouched mistaken refund, account for all recovered cash and use the guarded correction and linked replacement. Dependent activity needs owner reconciliation. Without an original receipt, do not invent a sale or false quantity; preserve the claim for review. Exchanges and price-only concessions need a separate agreed procedure.

## The printer fails or a receipt is requested again

1. Check Sales & receipts or Sync center for the already saved reference and total. A print failure does not reverse a sale.
2. Use the existing saved receipt to print again when the printer works; do not ring up another sale. Check printer paper, power and connection.
3. Record any manual customer acknowledgment with the same reference. The pilot's receipt is not an official tax invoice; agree the store's required invoicing process before real use.

## Close the drawer and hand over the store

1. Finish the basket and synchronize every completed sale. Resolve uncertain stock/owner requests through their original IDs; keep owner-review exceptions visible.
2. Count actual drawer cash, compare with expected cash and retain explanations for differences. Close the shift online. Do not change a stock count or create a refund to hide a cash difference.
3. Review expiry, held goods, in-transit transfers, overdue/unassigned investigations and pending issues. Assign a responsible employee and due date. Rehearse separate-person counts and keep signed daily cash/stock evidence.

## Storage is low, the PC is replaced or records need recovery

1. Synchronize and export every numbered recovery part while unresolved records exist. Keep the files private on a separate protected device; exports contain business records.
2. Do not clear site data, uninstall, use InPrivate or reset the PC as a sync fix. Preserve the original profile/device and ask the maintainer to recover space or data.
3. Use the same business/branch and authorized original identity to restore records on a replacement profile. Repeated imports and original retries retain identities. Operation-only files work with shifts closed; sale files require the authorized recovery procedure. Compare original IDs, cash, stock, held goods and outstanding transfers before retiring the original evidence.

## Update the app, add a branch or maintain it long term

1. Finish the basket and resolve pending records before Settings → Application & recovery → Check for app update. Reopen only when prompted and keep site data. One dedicated cashier window reduces sign-out and unfinished-basket confusion.
2. Owners add branches and assign individual staff roles in Settings. Verify real product conversions, lots, costs and independently counted opening stock; import each opening balance once. A new branch needs its own hardware and pilot checks.
3. Daily: review queues, cash, stock and investigations. Weekly: rotate high-value/loose-product counts and inspect storage headroom. Monthly: review recurring losses, access and capacity. Name a maintainer and backup owner; keep encrypted off-site backups and restore an actual production backup into an isolated working system. No automatic deletion of history, plan upgrade or backup scheduling is implied by these checks.
