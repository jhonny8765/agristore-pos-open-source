# AgriStore POS

## Platform
Web PWA: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase/PostgreSQL, Dexie, Workbox.

## People and purpose
Cashiers, branch managers, stock staff and owners at agricultural supply stores. Cash checkout, exact weight/pack conversions, traceable stock and recoverable synchronization across an expandable branch network.

## Constraints
PHP currency; Asia/Manila time. Online login/device setup/shift opening before brief offline use. Original sales and stock evidence remain immutable. Counts require two separate sealed counter identities and an independent approving owner. No credit, payment processing, marketing, AI, full accounting or official tax invoices.

## Public edition
Generic identity and synthetic examples only. No private company artwork, production credentials, store datasets or business incident records. Hardware and production backup acceptance remain unpassed.

## Stock follow-up workflow
Connected Inventory worklists show recorded expiry and unresolved investigations with explicit snapshot times. Owners and branch managers assign an active authorized employee and due date; assignees can record progress. Assignments and progress leave cases open and stock unchanged. Only the owner resolves an investigation with evidence. Staff/hardware and real opening-data acceptance remain separate.
